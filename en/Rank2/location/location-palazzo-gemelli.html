Wedding at Palazzo Gemelli, Orta San Giulio

Orta San Giulio, beside the parish church

Two frescoed rooms and a garden over the lake

The couple in these photographs own the Ristorante San Giulio in Orta: a wedding at home, in the village they know by heart, with the jetty and the motorboat at the bottom of the rise.

The palazzo tells its own story, arched courtyard, staircase and garden over the water: I work on the transitions, as the couple move through those spaces without stopping, and let the lake wind lift the veil.

Tell me about the wedding you have in mind at Palazzo Gemelli

A wedding in Orta San Giulio, in Doctor Gemelli's palazzo

Palazzo Gemelli was built in the early decades of the eighteenth century — and completed, like the neighbouring Palazzo Ubertini, in 1747 — commissioned by Doctor Paolo Gemelli, born in Orta and later court physician to Henry of Lorraine in Paris, then made a count by Carlo Ferdinando Gonzaga, Duke of Mantua. It stands beside the parish church of Santa Maria Assunta, along the same rise as the Motta.

Open in Google Maps

The building, late-Renaissance in layout, still bears frescoed mythological decorations along its facade cornice, likely the work of a Flemish school. Since 2012 its halls — two connecting frescoed rooms, seating up to seventy guests — and the garden overlooking the lake have hosted the wedding reception.

In a palazzo so rich in detail, I always look for a balance between the story of the rooms and that of the people: without one taking space away from the other.
